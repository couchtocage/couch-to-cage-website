Put CTC on your Home Screen

Get quick access to the homepage, Fighter Portal, upcoming fights, messages and travel details. The app is free to install; an internet connection is required for live information.

On iPhone, use Safari (not an in-app browser), then follow the picture guide. On Android use Chrome. If you already see an Install CTC button above, you can tap it.

iPhone / iPad — Safari

Step 1: Open in Safari. Go to couchtocage.com in Safari.

Step 2: Tap the Share icon. Look for the square with the upward arrow in Safari.

Step 3: Add to Home Screen. Scroll in the Share sheet, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Step 4: Tap Add. Keep the CTC name and tap Add. Open the new icon.

Android — Chrome

Step 1: Open in Chrome. Go to couchtocage.com using Google Chrome.

Step 2: Open the menu. Tap Chrome’s three-dot menu.

Step 3: Choose Install. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Step 4: Confirm install. Follow the prompt, then open the CTC icon.
